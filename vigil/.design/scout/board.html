VIGIL redesign: three directions

Full redesign of the VIGIL crew health app: precise and technical, dark default with a designed light theme, glanceable, few taps, plus a first-run tutorial that explains how it works and where to enter things. · Functional app (crew tablet and phone PWA)

Pick a direction (A, B or C), or mix ingredients across them. Swap any component by its code, for example "B, but use S3 instead of B2". Every direction is a blend: the final build combines the ideas into one original design.
A

Flight Deck

An instrument panel: one status readout you can read from across the cabin, then every vital as a calibrated gauge against your own baseline band.

It's the health app that reads like a spacecraft display: one glance tells you if you're go or no-go.

theme: Dark (light theme designed as a daylight panel)type: Display Space Grotesk / Text Inter / Numerals IBM Plex Monomotion: Signature: on saving a check-in, the status ring sweeps once and the tiles settle into their new state in sequence. Interaction: press-depth on big touch targets. Ambient: none, or a static dot grid only. Scroll: none; the dashboard fits one screen.

Blend recipe (ingredients, never a copy)

  1. gold: teenage-engineering → instrument-grade labelling: small caps units, tabular numerals, hairline rules
  2. godly: LocalCan → a terminal-green technical accent used only for live state
  3. godly: Griffin → dark layered panels with precise 1px borders instead of shadows
  4. 21st: Segmented LCD Number Ticker → readouts that change digit by digit, for vitals

What is new (not borrowed)

The Go / Watch / No-go readout: one word and one ring at the top, sized to read at arm's length, with the reason in one line under it. Status always has a word and a shape, never colour alone.

B

Procedure Card

The daily check-in as a NASA-style procedure: numbered steps, one action per screen, a cue card that tells you exactly what to measure and where it goes.

It's the health app that walks you through your check-in like a flight procedure, one step at a time.

theme: Light first (paper procedure), dark designed for sleep periodstype: Display Inter Tight / Text Inter / Step numbers JetBrains Monomotion: Signature: each step slides in from the right and the step rail fills as you go, ending on a one-screen summary. Interaction: large tap rows with a check mark that stamps in. Ambient: none.

Blend recipe (ingredients, never a copy)

  1. godly: Rerun → light technical layout with precise type and generous whitespace
  2. gold: stripe-press → editorial numbering and rules that make long content scannable
  3. 21st: Wizard Steps → a clickable progress rail with direction-aware transitions for the check-in
  4. 21st: Timeline → the daily routine as a vertical timeline with now-marker

What is new (not borrowed)

Every input step carries a cue card: what to measure, how (cuff, camera, wearable), and why it matters in space, so a crew member never wonders where a number goes.

C

Flight Deck + Procedures (recommended)

A fusion: the Flight Deck's one-glance dashboard for reading your status, and the Procedure Card's step-by-step flow for entering data. Read like an instrument, enter like a checklist.

It's the health app where you see go or no-go in one glance, and the daily check-in walks you through in under two minutes.

theme: Dark default + designed light (daylight panel)type: Display Space Grotesk / Text Inter / Numerals IBM Plex Monomotion: Signature: the status ring sweeps on save and the tiles update in sequence. Check-in steps slide with a filling step rail. Tutorial spotlight glides between real elements. Ambient: none. Everything has a reduced-motion fallback.

Palette

Hull#0b0f14 · ground
Panel#131a22 · ground
Instrument cyan#4fd1c5 · brand
Action blue#5b8cff · action
Caution amber#ffb020 · status
Readout white#e8eef4 · text

Blend recipe (ingredients, never a copy)

  1. gold: teenage-engineering → instrument labelling and tabular numerals
  2. gold: oura → a calm health app that leads with one readiness answer, then detail on demand
  3. godly: Griffin → dark layered panels, hairline borders
  4. 21st: Product Tour + Wizard Steps → spotlight tutorial on the real UI, and a stepped check-in

What is new (not borrowed)

A 'Today' screen with exactly three things: your status word with its reason, the one next action (Start check-in, Run reaction test, or Send report), and the body map. Everything else is one tap away.
