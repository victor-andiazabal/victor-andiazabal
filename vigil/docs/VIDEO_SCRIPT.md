# 30-second video script

Screen recording of the live app, voice-over, no music needed. Timings in seconds.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-4 | Title card: VIGIL, Les Tigres de Bethesda, NASA Space Apps 2026 | "On the way to Mars, a question to the flight surgeon takes up to forty-eight minutes round trip." |
| 4-9 | Dashboard, Commander, day 30, red "Act now" ring, tiles | "VIGIL lets each astronaut check their own health every day and see, in one screen, what needs attention." |
| 9-15 | Click the Sleep tile, trend chart with baseline band and 7-hour line | "Every indicator is judged against that person's own pre-flight baseline and against published limits." |
| 15-21 | Alerts screen, open the checklist on the reaction-time alert, source links visible | "Every alert says which rule fired, the numbers, the NASA or clinical source, and what to do next." |
| 21-26 | Reaction test running, tap on stimulus, result saved; then the flight surgeon message | "A sixty-second reaction test catches sleep loss early, and one message carries the whole day to the ground." |
| 26-30 | Settings: switch to Solar particle event, dashboard turns red; phone view; URL | "Station, transit, rover or base. Offline, on the device. Try it at the link." |

Recording tips: 1360 by 900 window, dark theme, Commander selected, scenario "Sleep debt and slowed
reactions", mission day 30. Use the "Early infection" scenario with Flight Engineer 1 if you want a fever
example instead.
